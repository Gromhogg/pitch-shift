Pitch Shift for Windows (beta)

Pitch Shift changes the pitch of what you hear, and of your voice in calls, live.

This is a beta. It has been tested on GitHub's Windows computers, not yet on many real PCs.
If something goes wrong, open "Pitch Shift Diagnostics" from the Start menu and send the
report it puts on your Desktop.


Pitch Shift Mic: VB-CABLE by VB-Audio

Your voice reaches Discord, Zoom and other call apps through VB-CABLE, a virtual audio cable
made by VB-Audio Software (www.vb-cable.com). Pitch Shift names it "Pitch Shift Mic".
If VB-CABLE isn't on this PC yet, Setup installs it from vb-audio.com. If VB-Audio's own
window opens, click "Install Driver".

VB-CABLE is donationware. If you keep using it, please consider supporting VB-Audio:
https://vb-audio.com/Cable/
