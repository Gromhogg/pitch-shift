Pitch Shift voice changer voices

Woman and Man voices: made by the Pitch Shift project from recordings in the CSTR VCTK Corpus 0.92
(Centre for Speech Technology Research, University of Edinburgh), licensed CC BY 4.0
(https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/3443). Each voice is a blend of 8 speakers; no single person is cloned.
Natural mode uses only each voice's average tone (long-term spectrum), measured from that corpus.

Realistic mode voice model: RVC v2 (Retrieval-based-Voice-Conversion-WebUI, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2023
liujing04, 源文雨, Ftps), fine-tuned from its pretrained 48 kHz base models (MIT).

Content encoder: ContentVec (auspicious3000/contentvec, MIT License), as distributed by RVC (hubert_base, MIT).

Converted to Core ML with 8-bit weights.
