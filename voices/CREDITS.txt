Pitch Shift voice changer voices

Woman and Man voices: made by the Pitch Shift project from the CSTR VCTK Corpus 0.92 (Centre for Speech Technology
Research, University of Edinburgh), licensed CC BY 4.0 (https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/3443). Each voice is
a blend of 8 of its speakers (Woman: the American speakers p294, p299, p300, p318, p333, p339, p341, p330; Man: p226,
p292, p260, p298, p302, p374, p232, p241); no single person is cloned. Natural mode uses only each voice's average tone (long-term spectrum).

Realistic mode (AI) voice model: RVC v2 (Retrieval-based-Voice-Conversion-WebUI, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2023
liujing04, 源文雨, Ftps). The voices are its pretrained 48 kHz base model (MIT), which was trained on VCTK, with the
speaker setting mixed from the 8 speakers above.

Content encoder: ContentVec (auspicious3000/contentvec, MIT License), as distributed by RVC (hubert_base, MIT).

Converted to Core ML with 8-bit weights.
