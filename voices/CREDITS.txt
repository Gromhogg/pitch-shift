Pitch Shift voice changer voices

Woman and Man voices: trained by the Pitch Shift project on the CSTR VCTK Corpus 0.92 (Yamagishi, Veaux, MacDonald;
Centre for Speech Technology Research, University of Edinburgh), licensed CC BY 4.0
(https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/3443). Each voice was trained on 8 of its speakers as one voice (Woman: the
American speakers p294, p299, p300, p318, p333, p339, p341, p330; Man: p226, p292, p260, p298, p302, p374, p232,
p241), about 40 minutes of their recordings per voice; no single person is cloned. Natural mode uses only each voice's
average tone (long-term spectrum).

Realistic mode (AI) voice model: RVC v2 (Retrieval-based-Voice-Conversion-WebUI, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2023
liujing04, 源文雨, Ftps). Training started from its pretrained 48 kHz base models (MIT), with the speaker setting mixed
from the 8 speakers above, and went on for 200 rounds (epochs) on their recordings. Pitch labels for training were made
with RMVPE (the RVC project's model file, MIT); RMVPE is not part of the app.

Content encoder: ContentVec (auspicious3000/contentvec, MIT License), as distributed by RVC (hubert_base, MIT).

Converted to Core ML with 8-bit weights.
