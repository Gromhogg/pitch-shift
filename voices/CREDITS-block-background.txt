Sounds used to train Pitch Shift's "Block background sounds" voices (3.5; woman_us_r4_ahead and man_r4_ahead).
These sounds were mixed UNDER the VCTK speech on the voice's input side only (the voice learned to make the clean
speech without them); they are never in the voice's output and are not part of the app. Plus made-up hiss and hum.
FSD50K: Fonseca, Favory, Pons, Font, Serra, "FSD50K: an open dataset of human-labeled sound events" (2020/2022),
dev split, clips from Freesound. CC BY: Creative Commons Attribution (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
and the 4.0 version); CC0: no rights reserved. VCTK: CSTR VCTK Corpus 0.92, University of Edinburgh, CC BY 4.0.
